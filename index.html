About Teacher

Meet Priyanka
Your Yoga Guide

With over 12 years of dedicated practice and teaching, I have guided thousands of students toward physical health, mental clarity, and spiritual balance. My journey began in Rishikesh and has taken me across India and internationally.

I specialize in Hatha, Vinyasa, and therapeutic yoga, with additional training in Ayurveda and mindfulness meditation. Every class is thoughtfully designed to be inclusive, safe, and transformative.

  • Iyengar Yoga Level II
  • Prenatal Yoga Certified
